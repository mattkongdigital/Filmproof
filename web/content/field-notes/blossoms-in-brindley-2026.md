Brindley Place has a small garden with blossom trees which bloom each spring.
When it does, people flock to see the bright pink petals and snatch photos
before they pass. Against the grey gravel, the red bricks of pavement, and the
office buildings, the blossoms really pop.

Each year, Brindley Place hosts a friendly competition where they encourage
photographers to share their blossom photos.

Up until now, I didn't think I had a camera good enough.

Here are my entries.
